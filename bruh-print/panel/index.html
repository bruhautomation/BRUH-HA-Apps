        What is loaded

        A LabelWriter cannot tell what stock is in it. Tell BRUH Print once and it will stop you printing a 2.25" label onto a 0.56" roll.

        Label stock

        If a label comes out sideways, press Edit and then "These are the wrong way round".

        Which measurement is which?

        Every label is two measurements, and they are not interchangeable. Across the print head is the width the head covers in one pass; along the roll is how far the paper travels for one label. Nothing can work out which is which for you — a LabelWriter feeds to the next die-cut gap and has no idea what shape the label was.

        A label much longer than it is wide is a wrap-around label, so its text runs along the roll automatically. Text direction is where you correct that for one stock, once — nothing asks again per print. Print the ruler on the card above is how you check any of it: it comes out with millimetre ticks on both edges, and you hold it against a real label.

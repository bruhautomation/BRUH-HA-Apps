BRigt light show director

Lab

Latency and feasibility probes for your actual house — LIFX round-trip times, sustained rates, an on-bulb waveform demo, and Home Assistant service-call timing. Coming in the next update; this build is the installable skeleton.

Light Map

Place each light, give it a role (candle, downlight, lamp, strip, party light, laser) and a spot in the house. The director choreographs from this map.

Library

Your music folder, analyzed: beats, sections, drops and synced lyrics per track.

Shows

Compiled shows — one per track, built ahead of time so playback is nothing but a clock and a cue list.

Calibrate

Measures how long your speaker takes from "play" to audible sound, using your phone's microphone — so the lights land on the beat instead of two seconds behind an AirPlay buffer.

Party

Pick a playlist and a speaker, press go. Or say "start party mode" and let the automation do it.

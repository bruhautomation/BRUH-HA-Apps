Party lights and lasers on smart plugs ride HA service calls, which are slower — this measures the real call-to-state round trip so their cues can be sent early by exactly that much. The probe toggles the device a few times and puts it back.

Light Map

Place each light, give it a role and a spot in the house. The director choreographs from this map: candles glow and never strobe, lamps and downlights carry the beat, strips carry sweeps, and party lights and lasers are saved for the moments that earn them.

The room

Tap a light to select it, then drag it where it is in the room (left ↔ right matters most — sweeps travel across x). The selected light can be re-roled or removed right here.

Library

Your music, analyzed ahead of time: tempo and the beat grid, song sections and drops, and time-synced lyrics where they exist. Analysis is the slow part of a show, done once per track — the show itself compiles from this. Add more folders with the add-on's Extra music folders option; each one is scanned all the way down, so subfolders need no entry of their own.

Calibrate

Measures how long your speaker takes from "play" to audible sound — so the lights land on the beat instead of two seconds behind an AirPlay buffer. Open this page on your phone, in the same room as the speaker.

Speaker

Start here if nothing has ever played. Test playback sends the click track and follows it the whole way — whether Home Assistant can find the file, what address it hands your speaker, and whether the speaker actually started — and names the step that broke.

Measure with this phone's microphone

Plays a 13-second click track through the speaker while this page listens. The clicks are unevenly spaced on purpose — the pattern only lines up one way, so the answer can't be off by a beat.

No microphone? Tap along instead

Plays the same clicks; you tap the big button on each one. Your reaction time rides along (~100ms), so prefer the microphone when you can.

Or type the delay in

A show will not start without a calibration profile, so one speaker that refuses to play the click track takes the whole add-on with it. This writes a profile you typed — stored as manual, because it was never measured. 0 is right for a wired speaker; around 2000 for AirPlay; measure properly when you can.

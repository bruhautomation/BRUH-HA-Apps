CHATEAU SW / EZ — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Flumioxazin .............................................. 51.0% (SW)
  Flumioxazin .............................................. 51.1% (EZ liquid)
  Other ingredients ........................................ remainder

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 59639-99 (Chateau SW), 59639-189 (Chateau EZ) — Valent
  HRAC group: 14 (PPO inhibitor)

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  Pre-emergence + early post-emergence herbicide for orchards
  (apples, pears, stone fruit), vineyards, blueberries, and other
  perennial crops. NOT a foliar spray on crops — soil-applied to
  controlled-spray strips on the orchard floor.

RATE (commercial broadcast)
  Pome fruit: 6 - 12 oz product per acre (Chateau SW); apply once
               in fall or once in spring, OR a split application.
               Max 12 oz per acre per year.
  Stone fruit: 6 - 12 oz product per acre per year. Trees must be
               2+ years established; apply only to dormant trees.
  Grapes: 6 - 12 oz per acre.
  Blueberry: 3 - 12 oz per acre.

  Treat orchard floor only — soil surface. Backpack equivalents are
  not appropriate; this is a banded soil application.

TIMING
  Apply to weed-free soil before annual weeds germinate.
  Best applied during dormancy (Nov - Mar) OR pre-budbreak in spring.
  Trees must be dormant; no green growth at spray site.
  Activation rainfall of 0.25-0.5" within 14 days improves control.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  60 days for fruit; do not apply within 60 days of harvest.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  12 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - PRE-EMERGENT ONLY. Do not apply over crop foliage or fruit.
  - Do not allow spray to contact green bark, suckers, or new shoots.
  - Apply when air is calm; flumioxazin drift is extremely phytotoxic
    to any green tissue.
  - Always read all use directions and restrictions in the registrant
    label before each application.

NOT FOLIAR — DO NOT MIX IN BACKPACK SPRAYER WITH FOLIAR PRODUCTS.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, shoes plus socks, chemical-resistant gloves.

FIRST AID
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If on skin: wash with soap and water.
  If swallowed: call poison control; do not induce vomiting.

OFFICIAL LABEL
  Obtain the binding label and SDS from Valent USA before use.

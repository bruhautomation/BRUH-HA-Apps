BONIDE FRUIT TREE SPRAY CONCENTRATE — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENTS
  Captan ................................................... 11.76%
  Malathion ................................................ 6.0%
  Carbaryl .................................................. 0.30%
  Other ingredients ........................................ 81.94%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 4-58 (Bonide)
  FRAC group: M4 (Captan).
  IRAC groups: 1B (Malathion), 1A (Carbaryl).

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  Multi-active ingredient homeowner spray for fruit trees and
  small fruits — controls a wide spectrum of common diseases AND
  insects in a single bottle:

  DISEASES:
    apple scab, cedar-apple rust, black rot, bitter rot,
    brown rot of stone fruit, peach leaf curl (early-season),
    fly speck, sooty blotch, anthracnose, leaf spots.

  INSECTS:
    aphids, codling moth, oriental fruit moth, plum curculio,
    Japanese beetle adults, leafrollers, cherry fruit fly,
    apple maggot, leafhoppers.

  Labeled for: apples, pears, peaches, nectarines, plums,
  cherries, apricots, blueberries, grapes, raspberries,
  blackberries, and strawberries.

RATE (homeowner per-gallon)
  3 tablespoons (1.5 fl oz) of product per gallon of water.
  Spray all surfaces to the point of just barely dripping.

TIMING
  Apply at 7 - 14 day intervals during the growing season:
    Apples / pears:  silver tip, pink, petal fall, then every 10-14
                     days through first cover sprays.
    Stone fruit:     bud swell, pink, petal fall, shuck split, then
                     every 10-14 days.
    Berries / grapes: bud break, then every 10-14 days through pre-
                     harvest interval.
  Do NOT apply during full bloom — malathion and carbaryl are
  highly toxic to pollinators.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  Apples, pears: 0 days for captan; 7 days for malathion governs.
  Peaches, nectarines, plums, cherries: 7 days.
  Berries: 7 days.
  Use 14 days as a conservative whole-product PHI.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  12 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - DO NOT apply during bloom. Highly toxic to bees from direct
    contact.
  - Do not combine with sulfur, lime, or strongly alkaline materials.
  - Do not feed treated fruit or foliage to livestock.
  - Carbaryl + Malathion: do not apply more than 7 times per year.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, shoes plus socks, chemical-resistant
  gloves, and eye protection.

FIRST AID
  If on skin: wash with soap and water.
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If swallowed: call poison control (1-800-222-1222).
  If inhaled: move to fresh air.

OFFICIAL LABEL
  See the Bonide product label and the SDS before use.

NATRIA NEEM OIL CONCENTRATE — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Clarified hydrophobic extract of neem oil ............... 70.0%
  Other ingredients ....................................... 30.0%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 70051-2-72155 (BioAdvanced / Natria)
  Mode of action: contact + smothering action; broad-spectrum
  fungistat / insecticide / miticide.

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  3-in-1 disease, insect, and mite control for fruits, vegetables,
  herbs, ornamentals, and shade trees. Controls aphids, mites,
  whiteflies, scale, mealybugs, beetles, leaf rollers; and the
  fungal diseases black spot, rust, powdery mildew, scab,
  anthracnose, and leaf spot.
  OMRI-listed for organic use.

RATE (homeowner per-gallon)
  2 tablespoons (1 fl oz) per gallon of water.
  Shake well; spray to thoroughly wet foliage including
  undersides.

TIMING
  Apply every 7 - 14 days during the growing season. Best applied
  in cool morning or late evening to avoid leaf burn and to
  protect pollinators. May be applied up to and on the day of
  harvest.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  0 days. Wash produce thoroughly before eating.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  4 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - Phytotoxic risk above 90°F or on stressed plants — spot test.
  - Do not combine with sulfur or copper within 14 days.
  - Avoid applying when bees are foraging.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, shoes plus socks; gloves recommended.

FIRST AID
  If on skin: wash with soap and water.
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If swallowed: call poison control (1-800-222-1222).

OFFICIAL LABEL
  See the Natria/BioAdvanced product label and the SDS before use.

FIRELINE 17 WP — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Oxytetracycline hydrochloride (calcium complex) ........... 17.0%
  Other ingredients .......................................... 83.0%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 80990-1 (AgroSource)
  Trade names: FireLine 17 WP, Mycoshield (Nufarm)
  FRAC group: 41 (tetracycline antibiotic, bactericide)

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  Foliar bactericide for fire blight (Erwinia amylovora) on apples and
  pears, and for bacterial spot on stone fruit (peach, nectarine).
  Sold for commercial use; not labeled for ornamentals.

RATE (commercial)
  Pome fruit (fire blight): 1.5 lb (24 oz) per acre at bloom
                            applied in 100 gal water = ~200 ppm oxytet.
  Stone fruit (bacterial spot): 1.0 lb per acre, 7-day intervals,
                                first cover through last cover.
  Tank-mix with a non-ionic spreader for best coverage.

TIMING
  Apply at early bloom; repeat every 3-5 days through petal fall.
  Pair with copper at green tip; do NOT mix copper into bloom sprays.
  Apply when temperatures are 60-90°F. Avoid full sun midday.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  60 days (pome fruit), 21 days (stone fruit).

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  12 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - Streptomycin and oxytetracycline rotation is mandatory in many
    states to manage Erwinia resistance.
  - Store cool and dry; the active ingredient breaks down rapidly above
    90°F or in direct sun.
  - Do not exceed 4 applications per season on pome fruit, 5 on stone.
  - Mix immediately before use; do not allow tank mix to stand.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, chemical-resistant gloves, eye protection.

FIRST AID
  If on skin: wash with soap and water for 15-20 minutes.
  If in eyes: rinse slowly with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If swallowed: call poison control immediately (1-800-222-1222).

OFFICIAL LABEL
  Obtain the binding label and SDS from the registrant before use.

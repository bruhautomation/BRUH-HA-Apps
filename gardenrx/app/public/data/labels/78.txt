KASUMIN 2L — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Kasugamycin (as hydrochloride hydrate) ................... 2.30%
  Other ingredients ........................................ 97.70%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 66330-404 (Arysta LifeScience / UPL)
  FRAC group: 24 (aminoglycoside, antibacterial)

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  Foliar bactericide for fire blight (Erwinia amylovora) on apple
  and pear, and for walnut blight (Xanthomonas) on walnut.
  Section 2(ee) and supplemental labels may exist; check state.

RATE
  Pome fruit (fire blight): 64 fl oz product per acre,
                            applied in 100+ gal water.
                            Backpack equivalent: ~0.5 fl oz / gal.
  Use up to 4 applications per season; rotate with copper or
  streptomycin to delay resistance.

TIMING
  Apply at 70% bloom and again at full bloom under high fire-blight
  pressure. Repeat at 3-5 day intervals during bloom if blight model
  indicates infection. Stop at petal fall.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  90 days.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  12 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - DO NOT use in or adjacent to greenhouses or nurseries.
  - DO NOT apply through irrigation systems.
  - DO NOT tank-mix with copper — antagonism reduces efficacy.
  - Resistance management: rotate FRAC groups; never use kasugamycin
    in back-to-back sprays.
  - Bee toxicity: low; nonetheless, avoid applying when bees forage.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, chemical-resistant gloves, eye protection.

FIRST AID
  If on skin: rinse skin with plenty of water.
  If in eyes: rinse cautiously with water for several minutes.
  If swallowed: rinse mouth, do NOT induce vomiting, call poison
                control (1-800-222-1222).

OFFICIAL LABEL
  Obtain the binding label and SDS from the registrant before use.

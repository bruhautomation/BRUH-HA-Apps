BONIDE NEEM OIL CONCENTRATE — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Clarified hydrophobic extract of neem oil ............... 70.0%
  Other ingredients ....................................... 30.0%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 70051-2-4 (Bonide / Certis)
  Mode of action: contact + ingestion; growth-regulator effects on
  many insects; broad-spectrum fungistat.

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  3-in-1: insecticide, miticide, and fungicide for fruits,
  vegetables, herbs, nuts, ornamentals, lawns, trees, and
  shrubs. Controls aphids, mites, whiteflies, scale, mealybugs,
  Japanese beetles (suppression), leaf rollers, leafminers; and
  fungal diseases such as black spot, rust, powdery mildew,
  scab, anthracnose, flower/twig blight, and leaf spot.
  OMRI-listed for organic use.

RATE (homeowner per-gallon, all crops)
  2 tablespoons (1 fl oz) of product per gallon of water.
  Shake well before each use.

TIMING
  Apply every 7 - 14 days during the growing season as a preventive.
  For active outbreaks, apply at 7-day intervals until controlled.
  Best applied early morning or late evening to avoid leaf burn
  and to protect pollinators.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  0 days — may be applied up to and on the day of harvest. Wash
  produce before eating.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  4 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - Phytotoxicity risk on heat-stressed plants or above 90°F —
    test on a small area first.
  - Do not apply to wilted or otherwise stressed plants.
  - Avoid applying to flowering plants while bees are foraging.
  - Do not combine with sulfur or copper within 14 days of each
    other — phytotoxicity.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, shoes plus socks; gloves recommended.

FIRST AID
  If on skin: wash with soap and water.
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If swallowed: call poison control (1-800-222-1222).

OFFICIAL LABEL
  Bonide product label is shipped with the bottle and online at
  the Bonide website.

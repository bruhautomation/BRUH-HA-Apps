ORTHO INSECT, MITE & DISEASE 3-IN-1 — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENTS
  Sulfur ................................................... 0.40%
  Pyrethrins ............................................... 0.02%
  Piperonyl butoxide ....................................... 0.20%
  Other ingredients ........................................ 99.38%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 239-2643 (The Scotts Company / Ortho)
  FRAC group: M2 (sulfur).
  IRAC group: 3A (pyrethrins).

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  Homeowner 3-in-1 spray for roses, flowers, vegetables, fruit
  trees, and shrubs. Controls:

  INSECTS / MITES:
    aphids, spider mites, whiteflies, mealybugs, scale crawlers,
    Japanese beetle adults, leafhoppers, leafminers, lacebugs,
    thrips, and many caterpillars.

  DISEASES:
    powdery mildew, rust, black spot, blights, leaf spots,
    scab — multi-site sulfur action.

  Sold as a Ready-To-Spray hose-end or Concentrate.

RATE (homeowner per-gallon, Concentrate)
  2 - 3 fl oz product per gallon of water.
  Spray to thoroughly wet foliage on tops and undersides.

TIMING
  Apply every 7 - 14 days as a preventive. Apply when temperatures
  are below 85°F — sulfur can burn foliage above 85°F.
  Begin at first sign of disease or pest pressure.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  0 days for most labeled crops.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  12 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - Do NOT apply within 14 days of an oil spray (sulfur + oil =
    phytotoxicity).
  - Do NOT apply above 85°F.
  - Toxic to bees; do not apply during bloom or while bees forage.
  - Pyrethrins degrade in sunlight; reapply if rapid pest rebound.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, shoes plus socks; gloves recommended.

FIRST AID
  If on skin: wash with soap and water.
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If swallowed: call poison control (1-800-222-1222).

OFFICIAL LABEL
  See the Ortho product label and the SDS before use.

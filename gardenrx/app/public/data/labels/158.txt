SCYTHE — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Pelargonic acid (nonanoic acid) and related fatty acids .. 57.0%
  Other ingredients ........................................ 43.0%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 10163-325 (Gowan Company)
  HRAC group: not classified (contact membrane disruptor)
  OMRI / organic-friendly: NO — Scythe is not OMRI-listed, but its
  mode of action is non-systemic.

SIGNAL WORD
  WARNING

USE
  Broad-spectrum, NON-SELECTIVE, contact post-emergence herbicide
  for orchard floors, vineyards, around buildings, walkways,
  and other non-crop areas. Burns down emerged annual and young
  perennial weeds within hours; no soil activity.

RATE (directed spray on orchard floor)
  Annual weeds, young growth:
    3 - 5% solution by volume in water.
    Backpack: 4 - 6 fl oz product per gallon of water.
  Tougher / mature weeds:
    7 - 10% solution by volume.
    Backpack: 9 - 13 fl oz product per gallon of water.
  Add a non-ionic surfactant at 0.25% v/v for best burn-down.

TIMING
  Spray on bright, warm (70°F+), sunny days for fastest activity.
  Re-treat new growth as needed; perennial weeds will regrow from
  roots — Scythe controls TOPS only.

DIRECTED SPRAY ONLY — DO NOT contact green stems, leaves, or fruit
of desirable plants. A drift onto bark or fruit will cause necrotic
spotting.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  Not applicable for directed orchard floor use (no crop contact).

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  12 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - Wear chemical-resistant clothing during mix/load — Scythe is
    corrosive and may cause severe eye damage.
  - Strong vinegar-like odor.
  - Do not apply to standing water or runoff areas.

PPE
  Goggles or face shield, chemical-resistant gloves, long sleeves,
  long pants, chemical-resistant footwear and apron when mixing.

FIRST AID
  If in eyes: rinse continuously with water for 15-20 minutes;
              call poison control immediately.
  If on skin: remove contaminated clothing; wash skin thoroughly.
  If swallowed: do NOT induce vomiting; call poison control.

OFFICIAL LABEL
  Obtain the binding label and SDS from Gowan before use.

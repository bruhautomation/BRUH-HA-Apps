GARDEN SAFE FUNGICIDE 3 CONCENTRATE — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Neem oil .................................................. 70.0%
  Other ingredients ......................................... 30.0%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 70051-2-71004 (Spectrum Brands / Garden Safe)
  Mode of action: contact fungicide, miticide, and insecticide
  (azadirachtin and related neem fatty acids).

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  3-in-1 spray for fungus, insects, and mites on listed fruit,
  vegetable, herb, and ornamental plants. Marketed as a softer,
  homeowner alternative to synthetic options. Controls black spot,
  rust, powdery mildew, downy mildew, anthracnose, scab, leaf
  spot, blight; and aphids, spider mites, whiteflies, beetles,
  caterpillars, scale.
  OMRI-listed for organic use.

RATE (homeowner per-gallon)
  2 tablespoons (1 fl oz) per gallon of water.
  Spray to thoroughly wet all plant surfaces, including the
  undersides of leaves.

TIMING
  Apply every 7 - 14 days as a preventive; every 7 days during
  outbreaks. Begin at the first sign of disease or insect
  activity. Apply early in the morning or late evening to avoid
  leaf burn and to protect pollinators.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  0 days.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  4 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - Phytotoxicity risk above 90°F or on heat/drought-stressed
    plants; test a small area first.
  - Do not combine with sulfur or copper within 14 days.
  - Avoid spraying open blossoms when bees are visiting.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, shoes plus socks; gloves recommended.

FIRST AID
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If on skin: wash with soap and water.
  If swallowed: call poison control (1-800-222-1222).

OFFICIAL LABEL
  See the product packaging or Spectrum Brands' website for the
  binding label.

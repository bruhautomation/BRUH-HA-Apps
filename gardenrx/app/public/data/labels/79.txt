ACTIGARD 50WG — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Acibenzolar-S-methyl ..................................... 50.0%
  Other ingredients ........................................ 50.0%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 100-922 (Syngenta)
  FRAC group: P1 (SAR / Plant defense activator)

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  A plant defense activator — induces Systemic Acquired Resistance
  (SAR). Used in commercial orchards as part of an integrated fire
  blight program. Also labeled for bacterial spot, walnut blight,
  downy mildew on tobacco/leafy vegetables, and certain other diseases.

RATE
  Pome fruit (apple, pear) for fire blight suppression:
    1.0 oz product per acre (commercial broadcast, in 100 gal water).
    Backpack equivalent: ~0.01 oz / gal (very small amount; weigh).
  Tomato (bacterial spot): 0.33 oz per acre, 7-day interval.

TIMING
  Pome fruit: start applications at first sign of bloom; continue at
    5-7 day intervals through the bloom period. Stops at petal fall.
  Build-up time: requires 3-5 days for SAR response to develop —
    apply BEFORE infection.
  DO NOT use as a standalone curative — combine with copper,
  streptomycin, or kasugamycin during the active infection window.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  60 days (pome fruit).

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  12 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - Phytotoxic at higher-than-label rates — never overdose.
  - Avoid use on weak or transplant-stressed trees; SAR diverts
    energy from growth.
  - Max 4 applications per season on pome fruit.
  - Compatible with most fungicides and insecticides; jar test before
    new tank mixes.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, shoes plus socks, chemical-resistant gloves.

FIRST AID
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If on skin: wash with soap and water.
  If swallowed: call poison control (1-800-222-1222); do not induce
                vomiting unless directed.

OFFICIAL LABEL
  Obtain the binding label and SDS from Syngenta before use.

SAFER BRAND GARDEN FUNGICIDE CONCENTRATE — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Sulfur ................................................... 16.0%
  Other ingredients ........................................ 84.0%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 42697-58 (Woodstream / Safer Brand)
  FRAC group: M2 (multi-site protectant, sulfur)
  OMRI-listed for organic use.

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  Contact, multi-site protectant fungicide AND miticide. Labeled
  for: roses, flowers, ornamentals, vegetables, small fruits,
  fruit trees, nut trees. Controls:
    powdery mildew, rust, black spot, scab, leaf spot, blight,
    brown rot of stone fruit, and certain mites.

RATE (homeowner per-gallon)
  3 - 6 tablespoons (1.5 - 3 fl oz) of product per gallon of water.
  Use the higher rate under heavy disease pressure or when growth
  is dense.

TIMING
  Apply every 7 - 10 days as a preventive during disease season.
  Continue applications until 0 days before harvest.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  0 days. Wash produce thoroughly before eating.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  24 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - DO NOT apply within 14 days BEFORE or AFTER an oil spray —
    sulfur + oil = severe phytotoxicity.
  - DO NOT apply when air temperature is above 85°F — sulfur
    burns foliage on hot days.
  - DO NOT use on sulfur-sensitive varieties: Concord and Niagara
    grapes, apricots, raspberries, Bosc pears, some apple cvs
    (Red Delicious, Stayman, Baldwin, King). Spot test if uncertain.
  - May damage some ornamentals; test on a small area first.
  - Yellow staining on clothing and concrete surfaces may occur.

PPE
  Goggles or face shield, chemical-resistant gloves, long sleeves,
  long pants, shoes plus socks.

FIRST AID
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If on skin: wash with soap and water.
  If swallowed: call poison control (1-800-222-1222).
  If inhaled: move to fresh air; if not breathing, call 911.

OFFICIAL LABEL
  See the Safer Brand product label and the SDS before use.

ALION — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Indaziflam ............................................... 19.05%
  Other ingredients ........................................ 80.95%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 432-1516 (Bayer CropScience)
  HRAC group: 29 (cellulose biosynthesis inhibitor)

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  PRE-EMERGENT, residual herbicide for the floor of established
  perennial crops: pome fruit, stone fruit, grapes, citrus, nuts,
  tree-nut crops, certain berries, and bearing/non-bearing
  ornamentals. Long-residual control (up to 6 months) of grass and
  broadleaf weeds germinating from seed.

RATE (commercial broadcast on orchard floor)
  Pome fruit, stone fruit, grapes, nuts:
    3.5 - 5.0 fl oz product per acre per application;
    Max 5.0 fl oz per acre per 12 months.
  Treat strip below trees only; do NOT broadcast across full canopy.

  Tank-mix with glyphosate or glufosinate to provide knockdown of
  emerged weeds (Alion has NO post-emergence activity).

TIMING
  Apply in fall (after harvest) OR in spring before weed seeds
  germinate. Trees must be well-established (3+ years old in most
  crops) — do NOT use on newly planted trees.
  Rainfall or irrigation of 0.5" within 21 days is needed to
  activate.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  14 days for most labeled crops; check label for each crop.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  12 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - NOT FOLIAR. Soil-applied only — do NOT apply over green crop
    foliage or onto fruit.
  - Highly mobile in coarse soils — do not use on sand with shallow
    rooting depth.
  - One application per 12-month period is typical; do not exceed
    label maximum.
  - Restricted in some states; consult state department of
    agriculture before use.
  - Drift to non-target plants will produce stunting of new shoots.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, shoes plus socks, chemical-resistant
  gloves when mixing/loading.

FIRST AID
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If on skin: wash with soap and water.
  If swallowed: call poison control; do not induce vomiting.

OFFICIAL LABEL
  Obtain the binding label and SDS from Bayer before use.

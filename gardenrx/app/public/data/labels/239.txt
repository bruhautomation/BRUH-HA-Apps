ORTHO MAX GARDEN DISEASE CONTROL — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Chlorothalonil ........................................... 29.6%
  Other ingredients ........................................ 70.4%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 239-2522 (The Scotts Company / Ortho)
  FRAC group: M5 (multi-site contact)

SIGNAL WORD
  WARNING

USE
  Broad-spectrum, multi-site PROTECTANT fungicide for control of
  many vegetable, fruit, and ornamental diseases — early and late
  blight on tomato, anthracnose, leaf spots, fruit rots, downy
  mildew, rusts, mummy berry on blueberry, Septoria on tomato,
  and many others. Best as a preventive — NOT systemic; will not
  cure infections that have already established.

  Important crop exclusion: chlorothalonil is NOT labeled for
  BEARING apple trees (post petal-fall). Use on apples is
  restricted to dormant-only or non-bearing trees.

RATE (homeowner per-gallon)
  1.5 - 3 fl oz (3 - 6 tablespoons) per gallon of water.
  Use higher rate under heavy disease pressure.
  Spray to thorough coverage; do not let solution run off.

TIMING
  Apply at 7 - 14 day intervals during disease-prone weather.
  Begin BEFORE symptoms appear; spray after disease shows will
  not eradicate, only slow further spread.
  Re-apply after heavy rainfall (rain-fast in ~2 hours).

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  Crop-dependent. Common PHIs:
    Tomato, pepper: 0 days.
    Cucurbits: 0 days.
    Stone fruit (peach, cherry, nectarine, plum): 1 day.
    Blueberry: 42 days.
    Carrot, celery, onion: 7 days.
  Read the specific PHI for your crop on the label.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  12 hours (homeowner pack).

PRECAUTIONS
  - Eye irritant — wear goggles when mixing/loading.
  - Toxic to fish; do not contaminate water.
  - Suspected carcinogen in some classifications; minimize exposure.
  - NOT for bearing apple trees.
  - Do not apply more than 7 times per season on most crops.

PPE
  Goggles or face shield, chemical-resistant gloves, long sleeves,
  long pants, shoes plus socks.

FIRST AID
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If on skin: wash with soap and water; remove contaminated clothing.
  If swallowed: call poison control; do not induce vomiting.
  If inhaled: move to fresh air; if not breathing, give CPR.

OFFICIAL LABEL
  See the product packaging or Ortho's website for the binding label.

BIOADVANCED FRUIT, CITRUS & VEGETABLE INSECT CONTROL — REFERENCE SUMMARY
==============================================
This file is an internal reference summary, not the official EPA label.
For the binding directions, consult the manufacturer's current label.

ACTIVE INGREDIENT
  Imidacloprid ............................................. 1.47%
  Other ingredients ........................................ 98.53%

EPA REGISTRATION
  EPA Reg. No.: 432-1502-72155 (Bayer/BioAdvanced)
  IRAC group: 4A (neonicotinoid)

SIGNAL WORD
  CAUTION

USE
  SYSTEMIC, soil-drench insecticide for foliar-feeding pests on
  listed fruit, citrus, and vegetable plants. Active ingredient
  is taken up through the roots and translocated upward; protects
  new growth for up to 8 - 10 weeks per application.

  Controls: aphids, whiteflies, leafhoppers, mealybugs, scale,
  Japanese beetle adults, lacebugs, certain caterpillars, plant
  bugs, root weevils, leafminers, thrips.

RATE — MEASURE BY CROP / TREE SIZE (homeowner)
  Fruit and citrus trees (apple, pear, cherry, plum, peach,
  nectarine, citrus): 1 fl oz product per 1 inch of trunk diameter
  measured at chest height, mixed in 1 gallon of water as a soil
  drench at the dripline.
  Small fruit (raspberry, blueberry, blackberry):
    1 oz product per 5 ft of row, mixed in 1 gal of water.
  Tomato, pepper, leafy / fruiting vegetables:
    0.5 oz product per plant, mixed in 1 gal of water.

  This is a SOIL DRENCH, not a foliar spray. Pour evenly around
  the plant's root zone after watering soil.

TIMING
  Apply once per year per crop. Pre-bloom (before flower opens) on
  fruit trees is preferred to protect pollinators.
  Allow 2 - 3 weeks for systemic uptake before pest pressure
  peaks.

PRE-HARVEST INTERVAL (PHI)
  21 days (apple, pear, stone fruit, citrus).
  21 days (cane berries, blueberries).
   7 days (tomato, pepper).
  Check the printed label for each crop.

RESTRICTED ENTRY INTERVAL (REI)
  12 hours.

PRECAUTIONS
  - NEONICOTINOID — highly toxic to bees if any product reaches
    flowers; apply ONLY before bloom or after petal fall.
  - Do not apply to crops with flowering weeds underneath.
  - Apply max ONE drench per calendar year on most crops.
  - Do not apply where roots overlap with neighboring non-target
    flowering plants.

PPE
  Long sleeves, long pants, shoes plus socks, chemical-resistant
  gloves.

FIRST AID
  If on skin: wash with soap and water.
  If in eyes: rinse with water for 15-20 minutes.
  If swallowed: call poison control (1-800-222-1222); do not
                induce vomiting.

OFFICIAL LABEL
  See the BioAdvanced/Bayer product label and the SDS before use.

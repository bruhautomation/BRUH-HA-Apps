# Design notes

Self-contained HTML pages (open them in a browser; nothing to build). They
are the roadmap the code is built against, written before the code, and
kept here so a future change can be checked against the reasoning rather
than reconstructed from it.

| Page | What it covers | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [brain-checks-and-self-tests.html](brain-checks-and-self-tests.html) | Deterministic house checks (findings that cost nothing), the in-situ self-test, and the feedback loop: run journal, diagnostics bundle, producer scorecard, corpus and replay. | Tiers 1–2 and part of 3 shipped in brAIn 1.29.0: `panel/checks/`, `panel/journal.py`, `/api/diagnostics` + the integration's diagnostics platform, the scorecard, `brain check`, `brain doctor --json`, `brain report`, `protected_entities`. 1.30.0 added the second set of checks (registry, radios, the machine underneath) and the Diagnostics section under ⚙. 1.31.0 added `sensor.brain_health` — the health entity from tier 4, on the usage tracker sensor's never-unavailable pattern, reading a verdict the panel derives once. 1.33.0 added the notification router (quiet hours, urgency per producer, a hold queue) so a finding reaches somebody at a time they can act on it. `brain doctor --deep`, rehearsal with planted defects, corpus and replay are not built yet. |
| [brain-capability-map.html](brain-capability-map.html) | About a hundred capabilities in sixteen themes that would make brAIn proactive rather than a reporter, with the platform enablers most of them stand on and a ranked top twelve. | Roadmap. The first forecast (`forecast.battery`) and the protected-entity policy shipped in 1.29.0; the **action miner** (`panel/actions.py`) and the day timeline (`see.timeline`, as the Activity tab) shipped in 1.31.0, along with the first thing built on the miner — an automation a person keeps undoing, which is the seed of override learning. **#4 (battery runway and device decline forecasts)** and **#5 (anomaly alerts through a notification router)** of the ranked twelve are done: the baseline engine landed in 1.32.0, and 1.33.0 added the drift `base.unusual` is structurally unable to see plus the router that keeps alerts from becoming noise. 1.34.0 was **#9 (override learning)** — the denominator `auto.overridden` shipped without, the conflict finding deferred when the override rules were written, and the override ledger that makes "you undo this every weekday morning" sayable at all. 1.35.0 is the second half of **#10**: `panel/rhythm.py` measures when a house actually gets up (the day timeline shipped in 1.31), and the morning brief is the first scheduled thing delivered on the house's own clock rather than a typed-in hour. 1.36.0 extends the baseline enabler the way the page itself specifies it ("numeric **and binary**"): `panel/closures.py` measures how much of each hour a door, window, lock or cover is normally open, and `evening.left_open` is the first check whose whole answer is a measurement of one particular house. 1.37.0 closes **#10**: the weekly report (`panel/weekly.py`) — energy against the week before, findings raised and answered, what was learned, and one thing to do — plus `panel/energy.py`, which reads Home Assistant's own Energy configuration rather than gathering up every sensor with a device class of `energy`, because that set double-counts by construction. 1.38.0 is the first two thirds of **#7**: `todo.brain` puts the open findings in Home Assistant's own To-do app and the companion app's notifications grow the tab's own endings as buttons, both answering through one request path so a tick teaches brAIn exactly what the button would have. 1.39.0 closes it: `panel/appliances.py` measures each machine's own bimodal power shape — including how long its quiet phases last, which is what tells a dry cycle from a finish — and `chore.waiting` is the washing that finished and is still in the machine, ending on the To-do list the release before built. 1.40.0 is the larger half of **#11**: `panel/thermal.py` measures how fast each room falls towards outdoors and how fast anything puts the heat back — the physics behind pre-heat, open-window detection and freeze protection, none of which had a number behind it — and the two findings it makes sayable are a room that never reaches what it is set to and a room that empties faster than the rest of the house. 1.41.0 closes it with what the model is *for*: `climate.preheat` (the heating starts too late, from the measured wake time and the room's own climb), `climate.window` (falling faster than the insulation allows) and `climate.freeze` (when the pipes get cold enough to matter). 1.42.0 built the **shadow runner** (`panel/shadow.py`: `time`/`state`/`numeric_state`/`template` triggers replayed over recorded history, anything else refused whole), the **proposal store and tab** (`panel/proposals.py`, the fifth kind of knowledge), **#8 history replay**, and the first producer — `panel/routines.py`, the habit miner behind **#2**. 1.44.0 finished #2's lifecycle, which 1.42.0 had shipped half of: `panel/trials.py` grades a trial as a replay of the week against the ledger of person-caused presses, and `panel/automation_writer.py` makes an accept append a real automation to `automations.yaml` — snapshotted into the edit journal first, reloaded, verified, reverted on failure, undoable from the toast, and refused for a protected entity because a panel-written file is not an MCP caller. 1.45.0 closes **#6** and **#3**, the two that make the house *act*: `panel/healing.py` is a closed playbook of three overnight remediations (start a `boot: auto` add-on that is stopped, ping a dead Z-Wave node, reload a config entry that failed setup) — off by default, once a night inside the quiet hours, three at most, never on a protected entity, never on a row a person has touched, no power-cycling, and no Claude run on that path at all, with the *next checks pass* as the only verification and the morning brief as the report; and `panel/playbooks.py` writes the emergency automation for smoke or CO, a water leak, and a freeze with the heating stopped, composed deterministically from the registries and offered as a proposal, with every entity it would act on listed by name, the protected ones shown as skipped, a rehearsal that executes nothing, no trial button (a replayed week held no emergency), and — against what this page says — **no unlocking of anything, ever**. What remains of the twelve: **#1** one-off intents and the scene-designer half of **#11**. Everything else is proposed. |

Check ids, thresholds and tier contents in the pages are proposals to be
edited; where the code disagrees with a page, the code is what shipped and
the page is what was intended.

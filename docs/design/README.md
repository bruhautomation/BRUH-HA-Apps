# Design notes

Self-contained HTML pages (open them in a browser; nothing to build). They
are the roadmap the code is built against, written before the code, and
kept here so a future change can be checked against the reasoning rather
than reconstructed from it.

| Page | What it covers | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [brain-checks-and-self-tests.html](brain-checks-and-self-tests.html) | Deterministic house checks (findings that cost nothing), the in-situ self-test, and the feedback loop: run journal, diagnostics bundle, producer scorecard, corpus and replay. | Tiers 1–2 and part of 3 shipped in brAIn 1.29.0: `panel/checks/`, `panel/journal.py`, `/api/diagnostics` + the integration's diagnostics platform, the scorecard, `brain check`, `brain doctor --json`, `brain report`, `protected_entities`. 1.30.0 added the second set of checks (registry, radios, the machine underneath) and the Diagnostics section under ⚙. 1.31.0 added `sensor.brain_health` — the health entity from tier 4, on the usage tracker sensor's never-unavailable pattern, reading a verdict the panel derives once. 1.33.0 added the notification router (quiet hours, urgency per producer, a hold queue) so a finding reaches somebody at a time they can act on it. `brain doctor --deep`, rehearsal with planted defects, corpus and replay are not built yet. |
| [brain-capability-map.html](brain-capability-map.html) | About a hundred capabilities in sixteen themes that would make brAIn proactive rather than a reporter, with the platform enablers most of them stand on and a ranked top twelve. | Roadmap. The first forecast (`forecast.battery`) and the protected-entity policy shipped in 1.29.0; the **action miner** (`panel/actions.py`) and the day timeline (`see.timeline`, as the Activity tab) shipped in 1.31.0, along with the first thing built on the miner — an automation a person keeps undoing, which is the seed of override learning. **#4 (battery runway and device decline forecasts)** and **#5 (anomaly alerts through a notification router)** of the ranked twelve are done: the baseline engine landed in 1.32.0, and 1.33.0 added the drift `base.unusual` is structurally unable to see plus the router that keeps alerts from becoming noise. 1.34.0 was **#9 (override learning)** — the denominator `auto.overridden` shipped without, the conflict finding deferred when the override rules were written, and the override ledger that makes "you undo this every weekday morning" sayable at all. 1.35.0 is the second half of **#10**: `panel/rhythm.py` measures when a house actually gets up (the day timeline shipped in 1.31), and the morning brief is the first scheduled thing delivered on the house's own clock rather than a typed-in hour. Everything above those on the list — one-off intents, routine mining with a trial, history replay, emergency playbooks — stands on the **shadow runner**, which is not built. Everything else is proposed. |

Check ids, thresholds and tier contents in the pages are proposals to be
edited; where the code disagrees with a page, the code is what shipped and
the page is what was intended.

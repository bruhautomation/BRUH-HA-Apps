Capability proposal · brAIn add-on

What a next-generation brAIn would do that today's cannot

About a hundred capabilities in sixteen themes, each with what it does, what it reads, whether it needs the model or just arithmetic, and which platform piece it stands on. Nothing on this list is something brAIn already does.

companion to “brAIn Checks & Self-Tests” · grounded in brAIn 1.28.10 · 2026-09-02

brAIn today is a very good reporter. It describes the house on a schedule, files what is broken, remembers what is true, and answers when spoken to. Every one of those is a look backwards, and every one waits to be asked or waits for its timer.

The next generation acts on three things brAIn does not yet hold: a baseline for every number in the house (so it can say unusual before it says broken), a record of what people do by hand (so it can propose what they would have automated), and a way to try a change without committing the house to it. With those three, most of what follows is a consumer, not a project.

The frame: two new kinds of knowledge

brAIn keeps three kinds of knowledge apart on purpose. The capabilities below need two more, each with its own store, lifecycle and one surface.

Memorywhat is true
Hypothesiswhat brAIn might have wrong
Findingwhat is broken
NEWForecastwhat is about to go wrong, and when
NEWProposalwhat could be better, and the change that makes it so

A forecast is a finding with a date on it and a confidence: “the back-door battery will be flat in about 9 days”. It lives on the Findings tab under its own filter, because the job is the same (a decision only the person can make), and it is scored the way findings are: did it come true?

A proposal is a change with a lifecycle: proposed → trialling → accepted | declined. An automation proposal runs in shadow for a week before anyone enables it; a dashboard proposal is a preview; a device proposal is a sentence with a reason. Declined ones carry a note, exactly as “Wrong” does, and the note teaches. Proposals get a tab of their own, because a list of things you might want beside a list of things that are broken makes both worse.

Eight pieces most of the capabilities stand on. Build these and the list mostly falls out; skip them and each capability re-implements a piece of one badly.

1Foresee: problems before they happen

The Findings tab reports what has already failed. Almost every failure in a house is preceded by a trend, and the trends are already in long-term statistics. This theme is the baseline engine plus a small set of physics.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
Battery runwayforecast.batteryFits the discharge slope of every battery entity over 60 days and files a forecast with a date: “Back door sensor: ~9 days left”. Batches by week so one Sunday message lists everything due before next Sunday. Adds the replacement to todo.brain with the cell type read from the device model.math
Device declineforecast.deviceRising frequency of short unavailabilities, Z-Wave retransmits or ZHA LQI falling week on week, a sensor's reporting interval stretching. Each is a precursor to the “unavailable for 24h” finding, filed days earlier with the trend as the evidence.math
HVAC degradationforecast.hvacRuntime per degree-hour of heating or cooling demand, tracked per month. A filter clogging or refrigerant loss shows up as the ratio drifting 15 % above its seasonal baseline before comfort suffers. The model turns the ratio into a sentence and a likely cause.math + model
Appliance wearforecast.applianceFridge and freezer compressor duty cycle rising, sump pump cycles per day climbing, water heater recovery time lengthening, washer cycle draw changing shape. Uses power sensors where they exist and door/temperature sensors where they do not.math
Sensor driftforecast.driftTwo sensors in one room diverging slowly, or one sensor whose daily range collapses over weeks. Catches the humidity sensor that reads 30 % forever and the thermostat that is 2 °C off from the room, before an automation makes a bad decision on it.math
Storage runwayforecast.diskGrowth curve of the recorder database and host disk, extrapolated to full. Names the recorder's noisiest entities as the fix.math
Anomaly alertsalert.anomalyAny entity outside its hour-of-week baseline by a wide margin: power draw with nobody home, water flow at 3 am, a door open four times longer than usual, a room cooling faster than it ever has (window open, or heating failed). Math finds it, the router decides whether it is worth a phone, the model writes the one sentence.math + model
Weather-ahead alertsalert.weatherForecast plus house model: freeze warning for rooms with exposed pipes and no heating call, wind warning with awnings out or covers open, heavy rain with windows open, heat wave with no cooling schedule. The action is a proposal, not an automatic close, unless the person has accepted that proposal before.math + model
Leaving and arriving checksalert.departureOn the last person leaving: garage open, door unlocked, oven on, windows open, lights on, water running. On the first arriving: what happened while you were out, in one line. Both learned from the action miner rather than configured.math + model
Consumablesforecast.consumableFilters (from HVAC runtime), water softener salt (from regeneration cycles), printer ink, vacuum bags, pool chemicals where sensors exist: a reorder date on the to-do list, with the part name from the device manual where the model can find it.math + model

2Propose: automations, scenes and dashboards the house is asking for

The single largest gap. People automate less than a tenth of what they do by hand, and the logbook already records every manual press with who made it and when. brAIn should read that and offer the automation, then prove it before turning it on.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
Routine miningpropose.routineFrom manual actions: “You turn the kitchen lights off by hand at 23:05 ± 12 min on 26 of the last 30 nights, always after the living-room TV goes off.” The miner finds the pattern and its best trigger (time, or the TV event, whichever predicts it better); the model writes the automation and the sentence. One press starts a trial.math + model
Trial before enablepropose.trialEvery proposed automation runs in shadow for seven days. The report says “it would have fired 6 times; on 5 of them you did the same thing by hand within 3 minutes; on 1 you did not.” Accepting enables it; declining files the note. No automation brAIn wrote is ever enabled without a trial unless the person says so.math
Override learningpropose.overrideWhen a person reverts an automation's action within minutes, repeatedly, the automation is wrong about something. “Evening lights is overridden 40 % of weekday evenings, always when the office is occupied.” Proposes the missing condition, with a trial.math + model
Scene miningpropose.sceneSets of states people put the house into together (four lights at 30 %, TV on, blinds down) that recur. Proposes the scene with a name and the trigger that usually precedes it.math + model
Automation from a sentencepropose.nl“When the dryer finishes, tell whoever is home.” The model writes it, explains it back in plain words, shows what it would have done last week from history, and starts the trial. The existing chat can do the first half; this is the lifecycle around it.model
Dashboard proposalspropose.dashboardEntities people touch most that are on no dashboard; a room with no view; a generated per-room or per-person dashboard from the house model, previewed before it is created. The card generator the insights already have becomes a dashboard generator.math + model
Device gapspropose.deviceRooms with lights but no presence sensing, a thermostat with no room sensor, an exterior door with no contact, a washer with no power monitoring: each named with the automations it would unlock. Not a shop, and never brand-specific; a sentence with a reason.math + model
Helper proposalspropose.helperA question asked twice in the chat that a template sensor would answer permanently (“how long has the garage been open”) becomes a proposed helper with its template.model
Standby hunterspropose.standbyDevices with a steady phantom draw and a switch in front of them, with the yearly cost and a proposed schedule. The proposal carries the number, because a number is what makes a person act.math
Blueprint extractionpropose.blueprintFour automations with the same shape and different entities become one blueprint and four instances. Offered, never applied.model

Everything brAIn produces is behind its own panel. A next-generation assistant is on the dashboard you already look at, in the to-do app on your phone, and on the speaker in the kitchen.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.

A to-do listsurface.todotodo.brain: every finding that needs hands, every consumable, every forecast due date, as items with due dates in HA's own To-do panel and mobile app. Completing one there ends the finding here. One list, two views.math
Actionable notificationssurface.actionsFindings and proposals arrive on the phone with buttons: Fix it, Not a problem, Try it for a week. The answer lands in the same store the panel writes.math
Spoken announcementssurface.ttsThrough the notification router: a brief on the kitchen speaker when the first person is up, a departure check at the door speaker, never during quiet hours, never to an empty room (the house model knows which rooms are occupied).math + model

Ask from anywheresurface.askA conversation entity that answers about the house with history, not only controls it: “how much did the heat pump cost last week”, “when did the garage last open”. The analyst path, exposed to Assist.model

4Understand the house as a place people live in

Memory is a document the model reads. A house model is a structure code can read, and it is what turns “the house” into rooms, people, routines and roles that every other capability can ask about.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
Occupancy modelhouse.occupancyPer-room occupancy inferred from motion, doors, media, power and device trackers, with confidence, as a set of sensors. Rooms with no presence hardware get an inference from their neighbours. This is what “never announce to an empty room” and “lights on in an empty room for 3 hours” both read.math
Routines per personhouse.routinesWake, leave, return, sleep, weekday and weekend, per person from their tracker and the rooms they touch. Kept in the house model, injected into prompts as a paragraph, and the reference every anomaly is measured against.math + model
Device roleshouse.rolesWhat each device is for: the freezer in the garage, the lamp that is a nightlight, the plug behind the TV. Learned from names, areas and behaviour; confirmed through hypotheses. Roles are what let the standby hunter skip the freezer.math + model
The house bookhouse.bookA generated, living document that explains the house to a person: how the heating works, what the automations do, where the shut-off valves are (if told), what to do when the alarm goes off. For a house-sitter, a partner, a new owner. Regenerated from the house model and memory; editable.model
Thermal modelhouse.thermalPer room: heating rate, cooling rate, solar gain by hour, response to outside temperature. Fitted from history. This is the foundation for pre-heat timing, open-window detection and the cost of a setpoint.math
Guest modehouse.guestWhen a guest is present (calendar, or told), relax the routines that would confuse them, pause anomaly alerts that are their doing, and generate a one-page guide for them.math + model

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.

Evening checkrhythm.eveningBefore the usual bedtime: what is open, unlocked, or on that usually is not; what tomorrow needs; one line.math + model

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
Tariff-aware schedulingenergy.tariffWith a tariff or price sensor: propose windows for the EV, dishwasher, washer, water heater, and the automation that moves them. Reports what last month would have saved.math + model
Disaggregationenergy.signaturesFrom whole-home power, name the big appliances by their signature (start surge, steady draw, cycle shape) where no per-device meter exists. Coarse, honest about confidence, good enough for “the dryer ran twice today”.math

Solar and battery strategyenergy.solarWith a solar forecast integration: what to run when the sun is up, when to hold the battery, what the export earned. Proposals, with numbers.math + model
Comfort mapclimate.comfortWhich rooms are outside their comfort range, when, and why (sun, draught, radiator undersized). Per-room, per-hour, from the thermal model and the house model's ranges.math

7Perceive: cameras, documents, sound

The MCP server can already fetch a camera snapshot. Nothing asks it a question. Every capability here is triggered by an event and answers one question once, because a camera on a timer is a bill.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
Visual verificationsee.verifyA contact sensor says the garage is open at 2 am; the camera says whether it is. A finding about a sensor gets a photograph beside it. Cross-checking a sensor against a lens is the highest-value use of vision in a house.model
Event questionssee.eventDoorbell: is there a package? Driveway motion: is that our car? Pet camera: is the dog on the sofa? Each is a trigger, a camera and a question, and the answer becomes a binary sensor an automation can use.model
Manuals and documentsread.docsDrop a device manual, a warranty or a utility bill into a folder; brAIn reads it once, files the durable facts (filter part number, service interval, tariff rates, warranty end) into memory and the calendar, and answers questions from it later.model
Device dossierread.deviceFor a device the registry names: what it can do that is not exposed, known firmware issues, the right battery, from the manufacturer's public documentation. Runs on request, cached per model.model
Sound eventshear.eventsWhere a microphone integration exists: smoke alarm, glass break, dog barking for ten minutes, a baby. Classified locally by the integration; brAIn decides who to tell and writes the narrative.math + model

8Safety and care

The capabilities where a missed alert costs more than a false one, so the router's floors are lower and the confirmation is two channels.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
Leak responsesafe.leakWater sensor plus a shut-off valve: propose the automation that closes it, with the narrative that names which sensor and where. Continuous flow on the meter at 3 am with nobody up is a leak nobody has a sensor for.math + model
Detector hygienesafe.detectorsSmoke and CO detectors: age from the device model, last test date, low battery, one that has gone silent. A calendar entry for the test.math
Wellbeing patternssafe.wellbeingOpt-in, for a person living alone or an elderly relative's house: no movement by their usual hour, the kettle not used, a door not opened. Alerts a named person, with what was and was not seen. The most careful capability on the list, with the most careful defaults.math + model
Freeze and heat protectionsafe.tempsRooms with pipes below 5 °C or a nursery above 26 °C, from the thermal model and forecast, before it happens.math
Air quality responsesafe.airCO₂ rising in an occupied bedroom, PM2.5 outside worse than inside: propose ventilation or its opposite, with the reason.math + model
Nobody-home security posturesafe.postureWhen away: which doors are unlocked, which cameras are off, whether the alarm is armed, whether any automation would open something. One line, then a proposal to fix the gaps.math + model

9Simulate and experiment

The difference between a suggestion and a decision is a number from history. The shadow runner gives that number for automations; the thermal model gives it for climate; a consented experiment gives it for everything else.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
History replaysim.replayAny automation, existing or proposed, run against the last 30 days of recorded events: how often it would have fired, at what times, and what it would have done to which entities. Answered in seconds, before anything is enabled.math
Conflict detectionsim.conflictTwo automations that would act on one entity in the same minute in opposite directions, found by replaying both. The existing “fighting” check finds it after the fact; this finds it before.math
Consented experimentssim.experiment“Try a 17 °C overnight setback for a week, then compare.” brAIn runs the change, measures cost and comfort against the baseline week, reports, and reverts unless told to keep it. Every experiment is bounded, reversible and announced.math + model
Change previewsim.previewBefore the fixer or an accepted proposal edits anything: the diff of the file, the entities it touches, and the replay of what it changes. Undo already exists; this is the look before.math

11Trust and control

An assistant that acts needs to be inspectable and boundable, or it gets switched off the first time it surprises someone. These are what make the rest acceptable.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
Protected entitiestrust.policyA page listing what brAIn may never touch and the ranges it may never leave, enforced in the MCP server for every path including the terminal's headless runs. Locks, alarms and garage doors protected by default.math
Activity ledgertrust.ledgerEverything brAIn did to the house, when, why, and how to undo it, as a sensor and a card. The action miner records it alongside human actions, so “why did that happen” can answer “brAIn did, because you accepted proposal 14”.math
Confidence everywheretrust.confidenceForecasts carry a probability, proposals carry their trial score, anomalies carry how far outside the baseline. Numbers a person can calibrate against, and the scorecard checks them.math
Cost per capabilitytrust.costThe usage popover itemises by capability, not only by run: what the morning brief costs a month, what vision costs, what a trial costs (nothing). Each capability has a switch beside its number.math
Per-person consenttrust.peopleRoutines and wellbeing patterns are about people. Each person in the house model can see what is inferred about them, and switch inference off for themselves.math
Quiet modetrust.quietOne switch that keeps every acting and alerting capability silent except critical safety, for the week the in-laws visit. Reported in the brief when it ends.math

What actually makes a smart home compelling

Before the second half of the list, the test each item was held to. A feature is compelling when it does one of five things; a feature that does none of them is a demo.

It disappearsthe house does the right thing and nobody touched anything
It keeps you safeand tells you the one thing that matters, once
It saves money you can seea number, with what you did to earn it
It lifts the mental loadthe laundry, the bins, the filter, the cleaner's code
It delightsa room that looks right at 9 pm, a year in review worth sharing

The first half of this list is mostly the second and third. What follows leans on the first, fourth and fifth, which are the ones the market has not delivered and which a model in the house makes reachable.

12Intents, not automations

An automation is a mechanism: if this, then that. What people actually want is an outcome: keep the office comfortable and cheap, make the house calm after nine, never let the basement get damp. brAIn can take the outcome and own the mechanism, and explain every act by the intent it serves. This is the single biggest conceptual shift on either page.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
Standing intentsintent.standing“Keep the nursery between 19 and 21 °C, quietly.” “The porch is lit whenever someone is expected after dark.” brAIn compiles an intent into ordinary automations (so they are inspectable and editable), keeps them tuned as the house and seasons change, and files every retune as a proposal with a trial. An intent has a plain-language owner and a budget; an automation has neither.model
One-off intentsintent.once“Turn the porch light off when the guests leave.” “Tell me when the laundry is done.” “Warm the office when I get home.” A voice or chat sentence becomes a listener that fires once and then is gone: no automation to clean up, no helper left behind. The most-wanted voice feature in any smart home, and the one that no automation model fits.math + model
Preference conflictsintent.conflictTwo people in one room want different things. The house model holds each person's ranges; brAIn resolves to the overlap, or to a stated rule (“the person who was there first”, “whoever is sleeping wins”), and says which rule it used.math + model
Scene designerintent.scenes“Design my evening for the living room.” From the fixtures it has (colour, white, dimmable, the lamp that is a nightlight), brAIn composes scenes for morning, day, evening and night with a preview, and a schedule that moves through them. BRight already does this for a party; this is the everyday version, and it is the feature people show their friends.model
Adaptive lighting, per personintent.lightColour temperature and brightness following the day is a solved problem; following the person in the room is not. The occupancy model and each person's preferences pick the curve; a manual adjustment teaches it for next time.math
Follow-meintent.followMusic, a podcast, or a lighting scene moves with the person from room to room, from the occupancy model, with a hand-off delay tuned by how often it is wrong.math
In-the-moment feedbackintent.feedbackAny act brAIn performs can be answered on the phone or by voice with “good” or “not that”, and the answer reaches the intent that caused it. The correction note that “Wrong” gives a finding, given to an action.math + model

13The house runs itself

The things a good building manager does at night and in an emergency, done by the add-on that already has the tools and the policy engine to be trusted with them.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
Overnight self-healingrun.healA playbook of safe remediations for known failure classes: reload an integration whose setup failed, ping and heal a dead Z-Wave node, power-cycle an unresponsive device that sits behind a smart plug, restart an add-on that stopped, clear a stuck automation. Each runs once, in a window, under the policy engine, with a rollback, and the morning brief says what was healed and what still needs a person. The house that fixes itself while you sleep.math + model
Emergency playbooksrun.emergencyPre-agreed responses to the events that matter: smoke or CO (lights full, doors unlocked, blinds up, HVAC off, everyone notified with the room), water leak (valve closed, water heater off), power outage on the UPS (non-essentials shed, router kept alive, a countdown), internet outage (local fallbacks, a note on the wall tablet), a freeze warning with the heating failed. Written by brAIn from what the house has, reviewed and accepted by the person, rehearsed on request. The one place where acting without asking is the right default.math + model
Vacation mode that learnsrun.vacationPresence simulation that replays the household's real routines from the house model rather than random timers, setbacks with freeze protection, deliveries watched, a daily one-line digest, and a warm house for the hour you land. Started by a calendar entry or a sentence.math + model
Temporary accessrun.accessThe cleaner comes Tuesdays 10 to 12, the dog walker at 1. A lock code that exists only in that window, created from the calendar, revoked after, with an alert for use outside it and a brief line when they came and went. Contractor mode does the same for a week, with cameras set the way you chose.math
Deliveriesrun.deliveriesDoorbell vision plus a parcel-tracking integration where one exists: “your parcel arrived at 14:02, it is on the porch”, and a nudge if it is still there after dark or rain is due.math + model
Sleep and wakerun.sleepA bed sensor or the phone's charger tells the house someone is down: the night path lights dimly on motion, the bedroom holds its CO₂ and temperature, the wake is a slow sunrise timed from the calendar's first event. Nobody configures a bedtime; the routine model already knows it.math

14Household and life

The mental load. Not one of these is technically hard; every one of them is something a person currently carries in their head, and the house has the data to carry it instead.

CapabilityWhat it does and readsMech.
Chore enginelife.choresChores that are triggered by the house rather than a calendar: the dishwasher finished (power signature) and has not been opened in an hour; the washer finished forty minutes ago and the dryer has not started; the bins go out tonight (council calendar) and the bin sensor has not moved; the plants' soil is dry. Each goes to the to-do list and to whoever is home and nearest, and is closed by the house noticing it was done.math + model
Appliance state trackinglife.appliancesWasher, dryer, dishwasher, oven and kettle as state machines from their power draw: idle, running, done, unloaded. Sensors an automation can use, and the foundation of the chore engine.math
Petslife.petsFeeder, litter box, pet door and tracker: “the dog has not been out since 7 am”, “the cat has not eaten today”, the feeder is low, the door was used at 3 am. Care patterns, with the same restraint as the wellbeing capability.math + model
Garden and irrigationlife.gardenEvapotranspiration from the weather integration, soil moisture where sensors exist, plant needs from what is planted (told once, or read from a garden app), frost protection for the tender beds, and a watering schedule that skips before rain and lengthens in a heat wave. Reports water used against what a timer would have used.math + model
Vehicles and charginglife.vehicleCharge to what tomorrow's calendar needs at the cheapest hours, warn when the range will not cover the trip, notice the car left unlocked in the drive, open the garage on arrival only for a known car (vision or tracker), and plug-in reminders when the routine model expects a long day.math + model
Water intelligencelife.waterWith a meter: usage split into irrigation, showers, laundry and unexplained, by flow signature; a shower that ran twice as long as usual; a toilet that runs; the dripping tap nobody hears.math
Asset registerlife.assetsEvery device with its model, install date (from the registry), purchase date and warranty end (from documents brAIn has read), firmware, and an age-based replacement window. The list an insurer asks for after a flood, and the source of “this is eight years old and failing”.math + model

If only twelve

Ordered by how much of the house each one changes for the effort, with the enabler it forces into existence.

1
One-off intents

“Turn the porch light off when the guests leave.” The sentence people already try to say to their house, and no automation model fits it. Small to build on the shadow runner; enormous in how the product feels.

2
Routine mining with a trial before enable

The action miner and the shadow runner, and the first time brAIn offers something rather than reports something.

3
Emergency playbooks

Smoke, leak, outage, freeze: written by brAIn from what the house has, accepted once, rehearsed on request. The capability that earns the right to act without asking.

4
Battery runway and device decline forecasts

The baseline engine, and the first findings with a date on them. Cheap, deterministic, and the thing every smart-home owner has wished for.

5
Anomaly alerts through a notification router

“Unusual” as a number, and a router that keeps it from becoming noise. Pays for itself the first time it catches water running at night.

6
Overnight self-healing

A playbook of safe remediations with rollback, and a morning line saying what was healed. The house that fixes itself while you sleep.

7
The to-do list, actionable notifications and the chore engine

brAIn in the To-do app and on the phone, with the laundry and the bins on it because the house noticed. Small to build, large in reach.

8
History replay for any automation

“What would this have done last month” in seconds. It makes proposals credible, migrations safe and conflicts visible before they happen.

9
Override learning and automation report cards

The automation you keep fighting, fixed by the evidence of you fighting it.

10
Morning brief, weekly report and the day timeline

Two scheduled runs and one picture that replace a dozen unread cards, delivered where people are.

11
Scene designer and the thermal model

The everyday version of BRight's director, and the physics that makes pre-heat and open-window detection possible. Delight and savings in one release.

12
Protected entities in the MCP server

The policy that lets everything above be switched on without a leap of faith. Not last because it matters least; last because it is the precondition for shipping the rest.

What this list is not

It is not a claim that every item belongs in one add-on. Vision, sound and wellbeing each deserve a switch that ships off. The order above is what would make brAIn feel like a different product within a few releases; the rest is the map of where it goes after that.

Companion to “brAIn Checks & Self-Tests”, which covers deterministic house checks, the in-situ self-test and the diagnostics loop; nothing there is repeated here. Identifiers such as propose.routine are proposals for names, not code.

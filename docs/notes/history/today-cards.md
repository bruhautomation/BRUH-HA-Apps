# brAIn: Today, the cards and their buttons: history moved out of docs/notes/today-cards.md

What each rule in `docs/notes/today-cards.md` cost to learn. None of it is needed to follow the rules; it is here for the next person who wonders why a press, a line or a count works the way it does. Pointers to tests under `tests/` name files that were removed in November 2026 with the old suite; the rules they enforced still hold.

## The screen

- **One card type.** The **Question** chip: a Yes / No / Snooze card wore Suggestion beside the proposals, two different asks under one word. `tests/test_cases_question_chip.py`.
- **The face of a card is what you need to answer it and nothing else**: kind, severity, name and room, a detail clamped at a sentence boundary with *More* (`DETAIL_GLANCE`; never truncated silently), one line of what to do, the plan if one is waiting, and the buttons — while what was read, what could be done, how sure and what looked at it first sit behind one `<details>` (`caseDetailsNode`, *Why brAIn thinks so*), closed by default and never rendered for a card whose only content would be the word *minor*. That UI is gone: `DETAIL_GLANCE`, `caseDetailsNode` and *Why brAIn thinks so* no longer exist in `app.js`; the redesign's one card type has a body clamped to three lines and one Details (`qDetails`).
- **A suggestion is a card in Today's queue.** It rode in the old feed as a case AND on a Proposals tab, so one decision was counted twice and answerable in two places with less evidence on one.
- **One list, one number on the page** (#554, `syncNeedsCount`, `needsCountNow`). The banner said "15 things need you" over groups that added to 16, because the badge came from the server and the group counts from the cards drawn, and an armed one-off was drawn under Chores and counted there. Now the tab's badge, the section's count, the line over the insight cards and the headings' counts are all read off `todayCards()` once its lists have arrived (the server's `queue_count` before that, the same derivation), and a card that is not a decision (`counted: false`, the armed one-off) sits in its own "Set to run once" group whose heading carries no count. `measure-today` holds that the four agree.
- **The sentence names what the number counts** (#1122). After #554 the badge, banner and group heads agreed, but the lead line still counted only problems and questions ("3 things need you now" under a badge of 5).
- **The feed sorts by band.** Sorting on the raw id put every proposal above every question, because a proposal's id is milliseconds and a hypothesis's is seconds. A unit mismatch posing as an ordering.
- **How soon to act** was #1327; the reading of food, water, heat or security through `corrections.record_floor` was #1320.

## The card's words

- **The fix block's heading.** It was "You'd need to" / "brAIn would" set as an uppercase label over a sentence that already opened with its own verb, then *How brAIn would fix it* / *How you'd fix it*. The button became **Go ahead** in #451 — it was **Fix** — and before that it was **Plan** — a bare press buying a read-only plan nobody had said anything about, which the homeowner never pressed. `tests/test_fix_instruction.py` held the box, its prefill and the `{change}` it posts.
- **Who acts.** The owner (#451): brAIn *"needs a way to decide if it can handle a task on its own … and when it can it really needs to be surfacing 'hey I can fix this, go ahead?' … It's kind of unclear when I need to do something vs it."* The card already decided whose move it was and said it only through a button's word. `tests/test_who_acts.py`.
- **The step a card shows** (#736): `tests/test_step_is_the_households.py`; a check's own sentences were held by `test_finding_words_sweep`.
- **A card is read by name.** A check's own sentence already used the friendly name; a Resident's claim, its fix and every evidence row named the id, and `light.laundry_room_countertop` is a thing a person translates before they can read the card.
- **A model-written row is stored in plain words** because a house's Resident cases still read `binary_sensor.x`, "04:00–14:00 UTC" and the add-on's Supervisor slug. `tests/test_model_words_plain.py`.
- **A placeholder** (#446): a fix read `add wait_template: "a template"`.
- **A step that hands the choice back** (#536): "either the service is due or the reminder was never reset" with a step for each, "also confirm the satellite's pipeline" about a setting brAIn reads, "put it back if asked". `tests/test_finding_one_step.py`. A check's own fixed wording was held by its tests.

## The answer row

- **A card's buttons are decided from the case.** The feed put *Do it · Not now · Wrong, because…* under every card, and three was the right number and the wrong three — reported as *"Yes/no questions have a 'do it' button. There are lots of buttons. I don't always see a dismiss. Some items are boiled down to 'change the batteries' but I'm getting a 'disable the integration' prompt."* Of its four rules, the second was **At most three visible presses and one is always a way to say no**, never behind the ⋯ and never absent from an answerable case; *Later* moved behind the ⋯ — and 2.5.2 reversed both of those (`MAX_VISIBLE` is 5 now; the way to say no stays). **The plan a fix run wrote was never on the case card**: `_FINDING_STATUS` folds `planned` into `open`, so the feed rendered *Do it* over a plan waiting for Apply. The planned card was then drawn with *Apply · Don't change it*; today it is Apply leading the problem row, and no *Don't change it* press exists.
- **One row of answers on every problem.** 2.5.0 gave each situation its own vocabulary — *Replaced it*, *It's back*, *It's off on purpose* — and what it taught was that the row changed from card to card and had to be read every time (*"Review cards — allow brAIn to fix them, say it isn't a problem, dismiss, move to the other to do list"*). That sentence is the row. The labels are one table because Repairs said *I've fixed it* and *Not a problem here* where the card said Done and Ignore — one decision in two vocabularies (#452). The redesign put Check again behind the ⋯ and Dismiss nowhere (*"the dismiss and check again buttons are useful"*). Home Assistant's Dismiss: *"I want it to dismiss the card in brAIn too… I don't need a memory for that"*. It was briefly the opposite (2.15.0: close the notification, ignore the Repairs entry, tell brAIn nothing), which made one word mean two things; the bus used to record a Dismiss press as a clear.
- **On a phone the answer row is one row** (`style.css`/`app.js` ≤640px). A problem's five presses wrapped onto two rows at 390px, so one urgent card filled the screen; below 640px *Check again* and *Dismiss* moved to the top of the card's ⋯. Then the one-row version cut "Add to To Do" with an ellipsis (#1123), and letting five whole labels wrap left one stray button on a second row (#1190), so the primary now takes its own full line. `measure-today`'s "wraps to 2 rows" lines predate this and are expected.
- **Every finding card has a Respond line.** The owner: *"These buttons don't cover what needs to happen here … Respond and I just type one quick instruction or response to brAIn and it either does that to fix it, write it to memory, or whatever."* It was first a one-line box under the answer row, "never a fourth button"; since #1190 it is the **Respond** press after the primary that opens that box (`addRespond`). `tests/test_panel_respond.py`.
- **The row is the same on every card.** A legacy step-only plan was said to offer **Fix** again; the code offers Go ahead (`answers._plan`'s default label).
- **Ignore chips.** The household ignored five of eight of the Resident's reports with a bare Ignore. `CHIP_NEVER_WORDS` held every `resident.NEVER_IGNORE` word by test (`tests/test_resident_ignore_chips.py`).
- **A Dismiss that names no length is the feed's Dismiss** (`finding_requests.parse` → `hours: None` → `cases.snooze_until`). The phone's Dismiss names no hours, so it used to get a flat 24h while the feed's gave one, three or seven days. One word meant three things. The phone's snooze button is now labelled Snooze, and Dismiss is the `dismiss` request that deletes the card.

## Questions

- **Doesn't apply.** The owner's words: *"it needs a dismiss option too because sometimes the question it asks is irrelevant."* `tests/test_question_not_applicable.py`.
- **A question brAIn asks.** Rows reading "Vesper asks: …" — the assistant's own questions, "Still open from an earlier conversation" — sat among the faults on Needs you and in `get_findings`, under Add to To Do and Ignore, with nowhere to type the answer. `tests/test_assistant_questions.py`.
- **A person's errand is answered with Done** (#1421). The owner's words: *"yes no is not the right response here. It's more like, okay, did that, we good."* A Resident `question` whose step was "look at the door sensor once: if it is shut, replace the battery; if ajar, close it" showed Yes · No · Snooze · Doesn't apply, and none of them is what the person did. The owner's reported row had a claim asked as a question, so it is Done. A legacy row whose step was a question and carried a `todo_text` had lost its Yes/No (#1427), and Morph's `done` press could reach `_end_finding` twice (#1427). Done gave a chore check's card its Done in Morph, where it had only Add to To Do.

## To Do

- **To Do is a section of Today.** It was a To-do tab with a count of its own, on the argument that an accepted chore is not a decision and the Findings badge should go down when one moved. The second half stands; the first no longer needs a tab. The add box's 16px was what `measure-todo` found on its first run.
- **The rows' presses.** Each row had two presses, **Done** (with its optional note into memory) and **Delete**, and a ⋯ holding Snooze and Ignore (the old Off the list) on its face. The in-place row (the owner, moving To Do off Morph's Feed, which is being removed) replaced them with a tick, and the presses moved into the opened task (`todoStory`).
- **A To Do card is one headline** (#879). The card showed the item's text and then its whole `fix`; 5.2.4 then composed a headline out of free text and was rejected three times ("Look at the Hall sensor: it battery low.", "…: hall sensor's battery is low.", "Look into: re-pair it.", "Hall sensor: 1." over "Replace the battery; 2; 3."), because every rule that rewrites somebody's sentence breaks on the next sentence.

## History and Changes brAIn made

- **Folds** (#1300). A card no store owns ignored through `today.hide` said a bare "Ignored", and a house with sixty ignored updates had sixty such rows.
- **The words the household was shown** (#1463).
- **Changes brAIn made.** The owner (#453): *"a running list of all the actions that brain has taken and changes it made. Each action should have an undo button next to it."* Everything brAIn changes was already written down and nothing read it back as one list; the `PreToolUse` hook began recording the `channel` for it. `tests/test_changes.py` drove the rules and the routes over the real hook, the real ledger writer and the real reverter.
- **The cases' count.** The read-model bullet said the badge is `cases.queue_count()`; the badge, `/api/status` and the mirror read `server._queue_count`, which adds the tidy table and every assessed update.

## Morph's bridge

- **The wire is files** came with Morph 1.0 / brAIn 3.0. Morph's presses were brought into Needs you's words in #452.
- **A suggestion is a QUESTION.** Filed as an `opportunity` with its ask in `fix` and `fixable: False`, it read as a pair of hands (Add to To Do · Snooze · Ignore) with no way to say yes, and Add to To Do told Morph `todo`. `tests/test_morph_suggestion_yes.py`.
- **A conversation task.** Leaving Morph's unclaimed filed every Morph conversation, and claiming its change requests as `automation` filed every Tell, among the person's own chats and Automation runs. `tests/test_morph_conversations_tagged.py`.

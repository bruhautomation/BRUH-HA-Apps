# brAIn: Today, the cards and their buttons

Today's layout, cases as a read-model, answers/situations and the button row, who-acts lines, To Do and History, Morph's bridge (feed.json, cases.json, replies).

Part of the project notes (see `CLAUDE.md` › *Where the notes are*). Read this file before changing what it covers; add a note here, not in `CLAUDE.md`.

- **The fix block's heading is one word, and the press under it says whose fix it is** (`app.js`'s `qFix` / `cases._base`'s `fixable`). It was "You'd need to" / "brAIn would" set as an uppercase label over a sentence that already opened with its own verb, then *How brAIn would fix it* / *How you'd fix it*; Today's card says **Fix** over two lines, and who does it is the primary press under it — **Go ahead** where brAIn could act, **Add to To Do** where hands are needed — so the heading cannot disagree with the button, and the who-acts line over both (next bullet) says it in words. `cases._base` still carries `fixable` (`_from_finding` honours `findings_store`'s rule: absent means fixable, only an explicit false means hands are required; `_base` defaults the other way, because no other store makes that promise). **The press does not claim the change happened**: `h_finding_fix` queues a read-only plan, the card shows *What Apply will change* with the steps and *What could go wrong* on its face, and only **Apply** changes anything. The flow is still called Fix it in `FIND_STATUS`, the fixer prompts and the server; the button is **Go ahead** (`answers.GO_AHEAD`, #451 — it was **Fix**, and the ⋯'s second try after a refused or failed plan still is), and before that it was **Plan** — a bare press buying a read-only plan nobody had said anything about, which the homeowner never pressed. **Go ahead opens a short box holding the card's own suggested change** (`answers._plan`'s `instruct` and `prefill`, the row's `fix` by friendly name; `instructThenFix` / `fixBoxOptions` in `app.js`, and the chat strip's Fix opens the same box), which the person may edit or replace; what is sent rides to the same route as `{change}` — `_run_plan`'s agreed change, the brief a discussion's `plan` resolution already sends — so the card then shows exactly what Apply will change and Undo follows. The wire id stays `fix` (the HA mirror, Repairs and the notification actions read it, and a phone is never offered it); the tidy situation's Go ahead is a bare press, because its answer is a table reviewed row by row. `tests/test_fix_instruction.py` and `measure-home` hold the box, its prefill and the `{change}` it posts. `measure-fixplan` drives the plan, the risk, the out-of-date and refused plans, the run in flight and what Undo puts back.
- **Every card on Needs you says who acts, in one line, and the line is read off its primary press** (`answers.who_acts` / `WHO_OF_VERB` / `who_line`, `server._who_for`, `qWho` in `app.js`). The owner (#451): brAIn *"needs a way to decide if it can handle a task on its own … and when it can it really needs to be surfacing 'hey I can fix this, go ahead?' … It's kind of unclear when I need to do something vs it."* The card already decided whose move it was — the primary press is `answers.answers`' — and said it only through a button's word. So each card carries ONE line under its title: **"brAIn can fix this."** over **Go ahead** or **Apply**, **"This needs you."** over **Add to To Do** or **Done**, **"brAIn needs an answer."** over **Yes** or **Send** (a suggestion "can set this up", a change brAIn made "made this change"). It is derived from the press rather than decided a second time from the case, which is what makes "never both and never neither" true by construction: one primary is one line, and a card nothing can be pressed on (a run in flight) carries none. `_cases_payload` stamps `who` on every case, and the cards no case covers get theirs from `_who_for` off the press they lead with (a loose finding, a guess, a suggestion, the tidy table, an assessed update), so the panel holds no table of it; the mirror carries the same line to Repairs (`answers.request_view`) and Morph's `cases.json` row carries it with `chip`. **An assessed update does not get Go ahead**: the brief allowed it for a `safe_tonight` verdict through a typed plan op, but an update card is not a finding and has no plan to hold one, and `plan_ops.REFUSED_DOMAINS` still refuses `update`, so it leads with Add to To Do and says "This needs you." `tests/test_who_acts.py`, `measure-today` (every answerable card, one line, matching primary) and `measure-home` hold it.
- **To Do is a section of Today, and it has no badge.** It was a To-do tab with a count of its own, on the argument that an accepted chore is not a decision and the Findings badge should go down when one moved. The second half stands — a chore is not in the queue and not in the count (`cases.FEED_KINDS`) — and the first no longer needs a tab: Today is the queue with To Do under it, so the work you agreed to is on the screen you open without a second badge competing with the one that means *waiting on you*. Each row has two presses, **Done** (with its optional note into memory) and **Delete** (`DELETE /api/todo/<id>`, `_drop_todo` without `ignore`: off the list, no memory line, no answer, and a moved finding's key released so it comes back only if brAIn sees it again — Dismiss's rule, on a chore), and a ⋯ holding **Snooze** (`/api/case/t:<id>/not_now`, the to-do store's own `snoozed_until`, hidden until it lapses) and **Ignore** (`/api/todo/<id>/ignore`, the old Off the list: a moved finding's key is released). Finished chores are History › Done with **Restore**. The add box (`#todoAdd`, *Add to To Do…*) is 16px on touch for the reason `measure-todo` found on its first run — `font: inherit` is class-qualified and outranks the bare 16px floor, so iOS zoomed the ingress frame in and never back out. `tests/manual/measure-todo.mjs` drives the real `renderTodo` behind `today-fixture.mjs`.
- **History › Changes brAIn made lists every change, newest first, each with its own Undo or the reason it has none, and never a second undo mechanism** (`panel/changes.py`, `GET /api/changes`, `POST /api/changes/edit|call/{ts}/undo`, `histFilters`/`changeMeta` in `app.js`). The owner (#453): *"a running list of all the actions that brain has taken and changes it made. Each action should have an undo button next to it."* Everything brAIn changes was already written down and nothing read it back as one list, so `changes.build` is pure over the stores the panel keeps: the edit journal (every tool, the panel's own writes included; the `PreToolUse` hook now records the `channel` that asked, the MCP server's reading of `BRAIN_CHANNEL`), the action ledger, plan-applied fixes still `fixed`, `tidy` batches, armed one-offs and accepted suggestions (a panel journal line matched to its settled suggestion by `ACCEPT_MATCH_S`, a one-off's own line folded into its row). Each row says what changed on what (friendly names off `_NAMES`, a file by its path under `/config`), when, and from where (`WHERE`: the chat, the terminal, by voice, a fix you approved, overnight repair…). **Every Undo is the one that already exists for its kind**: a file through `unfix.revert_edits` → `automation_writer.revert` (the reverter `brain undo` uses) and its reload, a service call through the restore press (`unfix.restore_plan` and `server._restore_calls`, lifted out of `h_finding_restore` so both doors run one implementation, never-restored rules and all), a tidy batch through `/api/tidy/undo/{batch}`, a one-off through its Remove, a fix through `/api/finding/{ts}/unfix` — and everything inside a fix's window is that fix's row, because its Undo puts all of it back. **Two rules keep it honest.** Only the newest change to a file or a device is undoable, because reverting an older edit throws away the later ones (the row says to undo the later one first); and a file whose modification time is past `OUTSIDE_EDIT_S` after brAIn's line, with no later line of brAIn's, was changed by somebody else and is not brAIn's to put back. A message, a call that named no single device and a before-state the chokepoint did not record each say why instead of offering Undo, and the route refuses whatever the list does not offer (`_offered`), so the button and the route cannot disagree. Thirty days (`CHANGES_DAYS`), fifty a page (`PAGE`, "Show older"). `tests/test_changes.py` drives the rules and the routes over the real hook, the real ledger writer and the real reverter; `measure-changes` fails on a row with no name, a row with neither Undo nor a reason, or sideways scroll at 390.
- **A card's buttons are decided from the case, once, and every surface renders that list** (`panel/answers.py` — `situation` / `answers` / `more` / `request_answers`; `cases.answers`, the mirror's `answers`, `repairs.FindingRepairFlow.menu`, `notify_router.actions_for`). The feed put *Do it · Not now · Wrong, because…* under every card, and three was the right number and the wrong three — reported as *"Yes/no questions have a 'do it' button. There are lots of buttons. I don't always see a dismiss. Some items are boiled down to 'change the batteries' but I'm getting a 'disable the integration' prompt."* Four rules. **A situation is a small closed vocabulary keyed on the check id, the kind, the store status and `fixable`** — `battery`, `unplugged`, `stuck`, `chore_check`, `automation`, `generic`, `hands`, `planned`, `planning`, `fixing`, `change`, `question`, `opportunity`, `chore`, `chore_done`, `watching` — named rather than derived from the sentence on the row (a derivation over words is a name gate, `dev.implausible`'s failure), and a check nobody has classified lands in the honest generic set. **At most three visible presses and one is always a way to say no**, never behind the ⋯ and never absent from an answerable case; *Later* moved behind the ⋯ — and 2.5.2 reversed both of those, see the next bullet. **A press that needs hands is never led by a press that needs a run**: `fixable` is the row's own claim, so a battery leads with the to-do list where a dead-ref automation leads with the plan run. And **every answer names its route and its wire action**, so `app.js` holds no table of what a verb does (`runAnswer` POSTs what it was handed, `askThenRun` opens the reason box with the situation's own `prefill` — *"It's unplugged or switched off on purpose."* — which on a `check:` row is the exception fact that stops the rule), the mirror carries `[{action, label}]`, the watcher stamps the action ids onto the issue's `data`, the Repairs flow shows that subset in `FLOW_ACTIONS` order with `DEFAULT_ACTIONS` as the floor for an issue an older add-on raised, and a notification's buttons are the same list capped at `MAX_ANSWER_BUTTONS` plus Reply. `todo` and `ack` joined `finding_requests.ACTIONS` for it — `todo` routes through `_move_finding_to_todo` and is deliberately not in `VERBS`, because it is not a settle-and-clear. **The module is a leaf** — `cases.py` and `findings_store.py` both read it, and a module both of those import may import neither — which is also why `request_answers` rebuilds a case shape off a bare store row: the mirror is written by the store and the store cannot ask `cases`. **The plan a fix run wrote was never on the case card**: `_FINDING_STATUS` folds `planned` into `open`, so the feed rendered *Do it* over a plan waiting for Apply, and the card now carries `finding_status`, `plan` and the fix window so `planned` renders the plan block with *Apply · Don't change it* and `fixing`/`planning` render a phase line rather than a row with nothing to press. `tests/manual/measure-home.mjs` drives the real renderer against the server's own shape (`answers`, `more`, `names`) and fails on a fourth button, an answerable card with no way to say no, *Do it* anywhere, a battery offering a plan run, a question without Yes/No, or a plan not on its card.
- **On a phone the answer row is one row** (`style.css`/`app.js` ≤640px, `measure-today`, `measure-home`, `measure-triage`). A problem's five presses wrapped onto two rows at 390px, so one urgent card filled the screen; below 640px *Check again* and *Dismiss* move to the top of the card's ⋯ (still at most three there) and the primary, Snooze, Ignore and ⋯ stay on the face — Ignore always, because an answerable card always shows a way to say no. An Urgent card carries a band down its left edge as well as the word. **A security or safety finding is a Problem whatever its severity** (`answers.chip`): `sec.*` checks file at `info`, and `info` used to mean Tidy-up, which folded them away with the unused helpers.
- **Every finding card has a Respond line, and it is Morph's reply line in the panel** (`server.h_case_respond` / `_panel_respond` / `_UNDO_SINK`, `interpret`'s `panel` surface, `addRespond` in `app.js`). *"These buttons don't cover what needs to happen here … Respond and I just type one quick instruction or response to brAIn and it either does that to fix it, write it to memory, or whatever."* A one-line box under the answer row (never a fourth button; 16px and a 44px Send on touch) posts to `/api/case/<id>/respond`, which starts `_morph_reply(case_id, text, "panel")` as a held task and the card polls `GET` for the answer: the interpreter's `panel` surface is Morph's vocabulary without `dashboard`/`code` (`PANEL_SCHEMA`), so a fix is PLANNED with the words as `change` (`consent`), "it's off on purpose, remember that" is Wrong with the reason (the correction and the exception rule; `SYSTEM` says so, and a `remember` beside a Wrong is not filed twice), and done, To Do, snooze or an answer in words otherwise. An ending's undo token goes to the reply's own result through a context variable rather than to Morph's Undo. The words and brAIn's sentence are the row's `thread` (cases carry the last four), painted on the card; the sentence is the toast. A house-book gap keeps its own answer box. **Ask on a report is a conversation** (`askAboutReport`, `askPrompt` + `openInTerminal`, the card as its first message); the card's dialog is ⋯ › **Edit**. `tests/test_panel_respond.py`, `measure-today`, `measure-cardhead`.
- **The row is the same on every card, including the ones no case covers** (`makeLooseFinding`, `answers.plan_refused`). A live finding the case list does not cover (one still waiting for a first look, or every row when the list could not be built) is drawn as the same card with **Add to To Do · Snooze · Ignore** and **Unchecked** on its meta line, never its own row of six. **Fix is withheld where a plan run already concluded brAIn will not make the change** (`plan_refused`, server-side and mirrored in JS), and the refused plan says *brAIn can't apply this* with its reason so its absence explains itself; a legacy step-only plan reads *This plan is out of date.* and offers **Fix** again.
- **One row of answers on every problem, and the situation decides the reason rather than the buttons** (`answers.HANDS` / `PREFILL` / `MAX_VISIBLE`). 2.5.0 gave each situation its own vocabulary — *Replaced it*, *It's back*, *It's off on purpose* — and what it taught was that the row changed from card to card and had to be read every time (*"Review cards — allow brAIn to fix them, say it isn't a problem, dismiss, move to the other to do list"*). That sentence is the row, in the redesign's vocabulary: **Go ahead** where brAIn could act (it opens the box with the card's suggestion — the fix block's bullet) (`fixable`, never on a `HANDS` situation) or **Apply** where a plan is waiting, else **Add to To Do**; then **Snooze** and **Ignore**, always those words and always that order. **The wire ids did not move** (`fix`, `todo`, `not_now`, `wrong`, `done`, `recheck`, `discuss`), so the HA mirror, Repairs, `finding_requests.ACTIONS` and the notification actions read exactly what they did; only the labels are new, and **they are ONE table** (`answers.REQUEST_LABELS`): the card's presses, `notify_router.ACTION_LABELS` on a phone and the Repairs dialog's menu all read it, because Repairs said *I've fixed it* and *Not a problem here* where the card said Done and Ignore — one decision in two vocabularies (#452). **Snooze is `not_now` and on every answerable card**; it settles nothing, brAIn picks when it comes back (`SNOOZE_BY_STAKES`) and the toast says when, off the press's own `snoozed_until`. **Ignore** is the one that is for good, and its box opens with the situation's own reason (`PREFILL`) under *Why? (helps brAIn learn)* and a tick, **Ignore all like this**, that sends the rule's mute (`/api/findings/mute`) beside the ending — only where `mutable`, never on a safety or Resident card. **A problem also shows *Check again* (a check's row) and *Dismiss* between the primary and the pair** (`answers._problem_row`, `MAX_VISIBLE` 5): the redesign put Check again behind the ⋯ and Dismiss nowhere, and the commonest card on a real house is one that is old or no longer true (*"the dismiss and check again buttons are useful"*). **Dismiss is `DELETE /api/finding/{ts}` and records nothing** — no memory line, no settled key, no rule, no capture label, `clear_resolved`'s ending reached by a press — because a card about a state that has passed is neither right nor wrong; it is refused (409) while a plan or fix run is on the row, and the toast's Undo restores the row. **Home Assistant's Dismiss is the same press** (`server._dismiss_finding`, one implementation): a phone's Dismiss button and the Repairs dialog's write a `dismiss` request (`finding_requests.ACTIONS`, deliberately absent from `VERBS` because it settles nothing) and the drain applies it — *"I want it to dismiss the card in brAIn too… I don't need a memory for that"* — so a transient card (a device offline for a minute) goes everywhere at once and comes back only if it is seen again. It was briefly the opposite (2.15.0: close the notification, ignore the Repairs entry, tell brAIn nothing), which made one word mean two things. The answer row carries no `request` for it because both HA surfaces append their own Dismiss to every row; the bus no longer records a Dismiss press as a clear, because it now arrives as an answer through the drain and counting both would be one press twice, and `notify_learn` counts a dismiss beside a snooze. *Done* (I've already fixed it) and *Ask* sit behind the ⋯, at most three. **A press answers with every list it moved** (`h_case_verb` merges the hook's payload under the feed) and `refreshToday` reads the cases, the findings, To Do, the proposals and Today's extras together before anything is painted, because a row moved by a press must not be drawn off a stale copy of the list it left. `measure-home` drives each kind's row and what each press sends.
- **A card is read by name, and the names come off the last checks pass** (`server._NAMES` / `_note_registry` / `_entity_names_in`, `names` on the feed payload, `prettyText` in `app.js`). A check's own sentence already used the friendly name; a Resident's claim, its fix and every evidence row named the id, and `light.laundry_room_countertop` is a thing a person translates before they can read the card. The map is built beside `_FACTS_CTX` from the snapshot the pass already fetched — never a registry read for the feed's own sake — and the panel substitutes every known id in the claim, the detail, the fix, the action labels and the evidence rows, keeping the id in a tooltip on the chip and the row; an id the snapshot never saw stays an id, which is honest. **The face of a card is what you need to answer it and nothing else**: kind, severity, name and room, a detail clamped at a sentence boundary with *More* (`DETAIL_GLANCE`; never truncated silently), one line of what to do, the plan if one is waiting, and the buttons — while what was read, what could be done, how sure and what looked at it first sit behind one `<details>` (`caseDetailsNode`, *Why brAIn thinks so*), closed by default and never rendered for a card whose only content would be the word *minor*. The Resident's contract asks for the same shape at the source: a claim under twenty words, three sentences of detail, friendly names in prose and ids only in `evidence`, no millisecond timestamps, and no action that amounts to *tell the homeowner* — filing the reply is how they are told. **And a model-written row is STORED in those words** (`panel/house_words.py`, `findings_store.plain` / `_plain_row` / `DISPLAY_NAMES` / `HOUSE_TZ`): a house's Resident cases still read `binary_sensor.x`, "04:00–14:00 UTC" and the add-on's Supervisor slug, so `add_case`, `add_many` (a source with no colon), `refine` and `set_fix` put an id the names map knows as its name, a UTC time in the house's zone and the slug as "brAIn" before the row is written — `text` only where it is the model's own claim (never a check's, never `SUBJECT_FOLD_EXCLUDED`'s), evidence never, and the correction, safety and security sources not at all. The panel hands the store `_NAMES` and the zone (`QUEUE_COUNT`'s arrangement); an id the map does not know stays an id. **A placeholder is refused at the same door** (`house_words.placeholder`, `findings_store.PLACEHOLDER_REFUSED`, `resident._no_placeholder`): `templates` turns template code into the words "a template", which is prose in a detail and a hole in a step, so a fix read `add wait_template: "a template"`; a detail, fix or To Do sentence holding a quoted or assigned "a template", `<entity>`, `YOUR_…`, TODO, xxx or an ellipsis standing for a value is dropped by `_plain_row` (so `refine` keeps the sentence it had), refused by `set_fix`, and dropped by `parse_case`, and the investigation's contract says a fix names real values (#446). The investigation's contract says the same at the source: local time and never "UTC", "brAIn" and never the slug, what an automation does in plain words and never its mechanics. `tests/test_model_words_plain.py`.
- **Cases are a READ-MODEL over the four stores, and an ending fires exactly one existing ending** (`panel/cases.py` / `CASE_HOOKS` / `h_case_verb`). Findings, hypotheses, proposals and chores stay in their own stores, mirrors and Repairs issues; `cases.list_cases` reads them into one vocabulary (`problem | opportunity | question | chore | change`) and three verbs (`do | not_now | wrong`), with the rare verbs under `overflow`. `cases.end` is synchronous and four of the endings are not, so a hook **answers with a thunk** and the route awaits it — `cases.end` still fires one per press. The helpers behind the hooks were lifted out of their routes rather than copied (`_move_finding_to_todo`, `_answer_hypothesis`, `_decide_proposal`, `_finish_todo`, `_drop_todo`), `_end_finding`'s reason: a case answered on the feed must teach brAIn what the same press on the old card did. `not_now` hands back no undo token because it took nothing away and the card says when it comes back; a hook that refuses answers in its own words as a 409 rather than a flattened 404. The badge is `cases.queue_count()` (findings + questions + suggestions), the one count — see *One source for every count*.
- **Morph is brAIn's face, and the wire is files** (`panel/morph_bridge.py`, `server._morph_loop`, Morph 1.0 / brAIn 3.0, docs/design/morph-vision.md §13–14). Morph's page runs in Home Assistant's frontend and its service is its own process (in brAIn's container since 4.0, docs/design/brain-morph-merge.md), and brAIn's panel port stays unpublished, so Morph reads two files under `/config/.brain/morph/` and writes onto the request queue every other surface already uses. **`feed.json`** is brAIn's measurements for Morph's salience engine (`morph_bridge.build_feed`, pure over the stores): `normal` is `[median, spread, samples]` for this hour of the week and the next off `baselines` — only a sensor with a real bucket for THIS hour, never the whole-history fallback, because Morph turns the spread into a deviation and a band nobody measured is a deviation nobody can trust; `doors` is `closures.usual_open`, absent rather than 0 for an hour not watched; `rhythm` is `rhythm.profile` in minutes; `habits` is `routines.mine` with `person: null` (the ledger records that a person did it, not which); `people` is one `/states` read. A store that could not be read is an ABSENT key, never an empty one. It is rewritten at startup, when the local hour changes and when a store's mtime moves (that is how "after the nightly build" is noticed without a hook in each build). **`cases.json`** is the one list (`_morph_cases_payload`): every live case `cases.list_cases(None, stores=STORES)` holds and `on_feed` shows, with its To Do sentence, its thread, a deterministic `ask` (`morph_bridge.ask_line`), Needs you's own status chip (`chip`, `answers.chip`) and who-acts line (`who`), the presses Morph can send (`morph_answers`: Fix/Apply, To Do, Dismiss, **Snooze**, **Check again** and **Ignore** — the presses Needs you offers in the same words, #452; `finding_requests.MORPH_ACTIONS` takes `recheck`, routed through `_recheck_finding` lifted out of the route, and `wrong`, the case's own Wrong with the request's `text` as the note, so it reaches `_end_finding`), `open` = `_queue_count` (the badge's number, so the two faces cannot disagree), To Do, `ended_morph` and `said`. It is rewritten within a second of any write to the findings or to-do store (`findings_store.ON_WRITE`/`todo_store.ON_WRITE` set a flag — never the publish, because a write must not wait on four stores being read), when any of the four stores' or the sidecars' mtimes move, and at least once a minute (a snooze lapsing changes the list with nothing written). One loop, supervised (`health.LOOPS["morph"]`), polls every second and does nothing on a dev checkout.
- **A finding is a conversation, and the words decide the ending** (`server._morph_reply` / `interpret`'s `morph` surface / `findings_store.append_thread`). A reply typed under a card in Morph is `{"kind": "morph", "action": "reply"}` on the queue; the drain starts it as a held task (`_MORPH_TASKS`, `_start_reply`'s reason) and it is read by the interpreter on the `morph` surface — the ask bar's kinds, every case ending, and three words no other surface has: `consent` (the card's primary press, read off `answers.answers` so a "yes" in Morph and the first button on Today cannot differ — a fix is PLANNED with the yes's instruction as `change`, never applied without Apply), `not_applicable`, and the Tell's `dashboard`/`code`, which ride on brAIn's turn as `tell` for Morph to run because brAIn cannot. The Morph-only words live in `MORPH_SCHEMA` (`interpret.schema_for`): every other surface is still validated against `SCHEMA` byte for byte, so a word only Morph may say cannot reach the ask bar's run. The person's words and brAIn's one-sentence account go on the row's `thread` (12 turns, locked `append_thread`); a guess, suggestion or chore has no row, so its thread is the side file (`morph_bridge.THREADS_FILE`), and a reply that ENDS a finding writes brAIn's last turn there seeded with the row's turns, published as `said`, because the row took its thread with it. **Nothing is guessed**: an interpreter that cannot run leaves the exact one-word answers a card invites (`_MORPH_PLAIN`) and otherwise a sentence asking again; a question back is one read-only run (`MORPH_REPLY_SYSTEM`, job `explain`) told to ask one question when the words are unclear. Every press — Dismiss, To Do, Snooze, Fix, Apply, Undo, Doesn't apply — goes through the panel's own helpers (`_dismiss_finding`, `_move_finding_to_todo`, `cases.end`/`CASE_HOOKS` via `_morph_end`, `_start_plan`/`_start_apply` lifted out of their routes, `_apply_undo` lifted out of `h_undo`), and Morph's Undo is the last token a Morph press handed back for that case (`MORPH_UNDO`), never somebody else's.
- **Morph's suggestions are a producer, and their answer goes back to Morph rather than into memory** (`_apply_morph_case` / `_end_morph_case` / `morph_bridge.note_ended`). Morph keeps no list: its learning suggestions are `morph_case` requests filed through `triage.gate` (which is what drops them when somebody pressed *Stop raising these*) and then `findings_store.add_case`, because Morph's pass IS the look — a suggestion about a page is not a reading a first look could judge. The row carries `morph_key`; `morph` is in `SUBJECT_FOLD_EXCLUDED` so it never folds into another producer's row about the same entity. Any ending given anywhere — Today, a phone, Repairs, a reply — reaches `_end_finding`, which hands a `source: morph` row to `_end_morph_case`: the row is settled (so it is not filed again) and NOTHING is written to memory, labelled or made a rule, because where a card sits on a page is Morph's to learn; the answer is recorded as `ended_morph` (`accepted|declined|dismissed|todo`, `/data/morph-ended.json`, last 50), and brAIn does nothing to the dashboard — Morph runs its own Tell. A Morph `withdraw` is `clear_resolved`'s ending and tells Morph nothing. **A suggestion is a QUESTION** (`answers.morph_question` / `as_morph_question`): filed as an `opportunity` with its ask in `fix` and `fixable: False`, it read as a pair of hands (Add to To Do · Snooze · Ignore) with no way to say yes, and Add to To Do told Morph `todo`.

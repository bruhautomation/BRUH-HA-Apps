Touch
  • One finger — turn the part
  • Two fingers — slide it around
  • Pinch — zoom toward your fingers
  • Measure — tap two points on the part
Mouse
  • Drag or Shift + middle-drag — turn the part, about the point you grab
  • Middle-drag or right-drag — slide it around
  • Scroll — zoom toward the pointer
  • Double-click — put that point in the middle

Home goes back to the corner view with the whole part in sight; Top, Front, Right, Iso turn to that side. Tap a body's name below to hide or show it, and its dot to change its colour. Which way a vertical drag turns is in Settings.
